# ADR Reporting Workflow

## Step 1 — Identify the patient
Record basic demographic information while protecting confidentiality.

## Step 2 — Identify the suspected medicine
Record the medicine name, dose, route, frequency, indication and relevant dates.

## Step 3 — Describe the adverse reaction
Document the event using clear clinical terminology and record onset and recovery dates where known.

## Step 4 — Document outcome and seriousness
Record whether the patient recovered, is recovering, has not recovered, or experienced another relevant outcome. Seriousness should be assessed using the applicable pharmacovigilance criteria rather than severity alone.

## Step 5 — Record the reporter
Document the reporter's identity, professional role and contact details as required by the reporting system.

## Step 6 — Add supporting information
Include relevant medical history, concomitant medicines, investigations, treatment, dechallenge/rechallenge information and other clinically useful details.

## Step 7 — Review
Check the report for completeness, consistency, accuracy and confidentiality before submission.

## Step 8 — Submit
In real practice, submit the report through the applicable institutional, national or regulatory pharmacovigilance reporting pathway.
